# Data sources

## Template knee geometry and kinematics (embedded in index.html)

Andreassen T, Hume D, Hamilton L, Hegg S, Higinbotham S, Shelburne K. *Subject-specific knee models, data, and results for specimen S193761.* Dryad, 2025. https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.zcrjdfnpv — licence: CC0 (public domain).

The dataset authors ask users to cite:
- Andreassen TE et al. Apparatus for In Vivo Knee Laxity Assessment Using High-Speed Stereo Radiography. *J Med Devices* 2021;15(4):041004. https://doi.org/10.1115/1.4051834
- Andreassen TE et al. Validation of Subject-Specific Knee Models from In Vivo Measurements. *Front Bioeng Biotechnol* 2025. https://doi.org/10.3389/fbioe.2025.1554836

Used: MRI segmentations (STL), colour surface scans (OBJ, baked to vertex colours), passive-flexion and internal-rotation laxity data (RKS, filtered).

## Vietnamese reference anatomy (embedded as aggregate values)

Cadaver study of the iliotibial band and anterolateral ligament in 22 formalin-fixed Vietnamese knees (NCS Đoàn Việt Hùng and colleagues). Only aggregate values (means, SDs, proportions) and measurement reliability are embedded; no individual specimen data or donor information are included.
**Before making this repository public, confirm permission and the preferred citation with the study authors and update this section.**

## Literature parameters

Rotational model and literature checks use published values from, among others: Geeslin 2017/2018 (AJSM), Huser 2017 (Arthroscopy), Noyes 2017 (AJSM), Delaloye 2020 (Arthroscopy), Lee 2023 (Arthroscopy), Rasmussen 2016 (AJSM), Jette 2019 (Knee), Helito 2014 (OJSM), Zens 2015 (AJSM), Imbert 2016 (Arthroscopy), Dodds 2014 (Bone Joint J), Kittl 2015 (AJSM), Kennedy 2015 (AJSM), Claes 2013 (J Anat), Wojtys 2003 (JBJS), Hsu 2006 (AJSM), Koga 2010 (AJSM), Getgood 2019 (KSSTA) and 2020 (AJSM), Sonnery-Cottet 2017 (J Orthop Traumatol). See docs/METHODS.md.

## Test data (not included)

The patient-scan module was tested on MRI series from the University of Leeds knee dataset (Wijayathunga N, Day GA, Jones A, Wilcox R, Mengoni M. *High resolution CT and MR images of cadaveric knees – dataset 05 (LTKN9347)*, 2026, https://doi.org/10.5518/1906, CC BY 4.0). These images are not redistributed here.
